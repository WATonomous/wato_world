# Proposal generation, shape completion and multi-source box fusion for offline 3D auto-labeling

Scope: how offline / unsupervised 3D auto-labelers turn LiDAR clusters, camera-lifted instances and other sources into 3D box proposals; how they complete the shape of sparse or one-sided objects; how they fuse sources; how they reject false proposals; and whether box fitting belongs at the frame or the track level. Written against wato_world's proposal_generation problems: far lifted car boxes covering one side (median 15 points; l/w/h 2.26/0.83/1.42 m vs 3.96/1.68/1.59 for fused pairs), lifted car boxes up to 3.28 m tall (bleed), a hidden car boxed on its occluder, and little per-frame overlap between sources. Researched 2026-11-02. Years are first-arXiv / venue years. "Inference" bullets are mine, not the sources'.

## Q1. Box fitting from partial point sets: L-shape, min-area vs search fitting, size priors, completion, prototypes, shape-prior fitting. Which works best for far, one-sided cars, and by how much?

### Takeaway
No paper compares these head to head on far, one-sided cars. The cheapest fixes with the largest reported gains are training-free: (a) **anchor-and-resize**. Replace the observed l/w with a class or track size and re-anchor the box on its best-observed corner or edge, not its centroid. CPD's version gives +19.3 vehicle 3D AP (most of it from re-localisation, not re-sizing); OYSTER's track-level variant gives +4.9 AP@0.7; VESPA's "inflate away from ego" step is worth 6 mAP. (b) **Fit size once per track or per static object** rather than per frame (3DAL: static-vehicle box accuracy 67.2 → 82.3 at IoU 0.7). Learned point completion (ZOPP) and SDF shape-prior fitting (SLF) help more but cost more: ZOPP needs a trained completion network, and SLF degrades with fewer beams and has no per-distance numbers.

### Cited Findings
**L-shape / rectangle fitting (the base step everyone uses)**
- Zhang et al., "Efficient L-Shape Fitting for Vehicle Detection Using Laser Scanners" (IV 2017) treats fitting as a search over headings scored by one of three criteria: area minimisation, closeness maximisation and variance minimisation. Per the authors' slides, closeness and variance were the most reliable (both ahead of PCA). Area minimisation was reliable mainly on dense returns. Runtimes were about 3.6 / 4.0 / 8.4 ms (area / closeness / variance). — [IV 2017 slides](https://shawxiaozhang.github.io/slides/2017_IV_slides.pdf); [CMU RI page](https://www.ri.cmu.edu/publications/efficient-l-shape-fitting-for-vehicle-detection-using-laser-scanners/) (taken from a search summary of the slides; I did not read the slides directly)
- The closeness criterion can settle on a wrong pose for a sparse L-shaped cluster and stays limited for occluded clusters; a convex-hull method did better on those cases (2023). — [arXiv 2302.01034](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.01034)
- The PythonRobotics reference implementation offers AREA / CLOSENESS / VARIANCE and defaults to VARIANCE. — [PythonRobotics rectangle_fitting](https://atsushisakai.github.io/PythonRobotics/_modules/Mapping/rectangle_fitting/rectangle_fitting.html)
- Zhang-2017 L-shape fitting is the box fitter in VESPA, CPD (MFC stage), ZOPP, LiSe's LiDAR branch and DFU3D. — [VESPA](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.20397), [CPD](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16493), [ZOPP](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.05311), [LiSe](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.08569v1), [DFU3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.20530)
- Fitters that keep the tight extent: OpenSight fits an **axis-aligned** box to the densest region-grown cluster in each frustum, and LiSe's image branch fits "a tight external 3D box". — [OpenSight](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.08876v1), [LiSe](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.08569v1)
- UniLiPs fits a minimum-enclosing cuboid with a minimum size enforced (value not stated) and PCA initial yaw. — [UniLiPs](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05105)
- OYSTER states the failure directly: "Due to the tightest-box-fitting nature of the seed clustering labels, the bbox sizes … are smaller for partially observed objects, especially for those farther away from the ego-vehicle." — [OYSTER, CVPR 2023](https://research-assets.waabi.ai/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Oyster-paper.pdf)

**Search-based fitting with size priors**
- Find n' Propagate (arXiv 2403.13556, 2024; "Greedy Box Seeker") searches each 2D-box frustum over k_d depth × k_s scale × k_o orientation (0–π) candidates. Anchors are per-class OpenPCDet sizes scaled by γ ∈ (0.95, 1.2), with depth bounds from frustum-point quantiles. Each candidate is scored C = C1 + α·C2: C1 is points inside divided by the maximum over the frustum's candidates, C2 is the IoU of the projected 3D box with the 2D box. The argmax per frustum is kept. Oracle ablation (NDS / APnovel / ARnovel): C1 only 20.69 / 15.97 / 24.08; C2 only 21.02 / 15.05 / 21.82; both 22.40 / 16.72 / 26.70. The k values and α are not reported, nor are per-distance results. — [Find n' Propagate](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.13556)

**Class size priors (hand-set, LLM-derived)**
- VESPA (arXiv 2507.20397, July 2025, preprint from a master's thesis) asks an LLM for three things: average class width, used as the XY-DBSCAN eps (keep the largest component); maximum urban velocity, used as a matching gate; and class size priors. Boxes with "excessively small" dimensions are **inflated to class means, shifting the box outward away from ego** to keep the LiDAR-observed surface aligned. Heading follows motion above 0.5 m/s. Ablation on nuScenes val (3 classes): full 46.76 mAP / 43.47 NDS; without box inflation 40.68 / 35.71; without tracking 25.48 / 21.84; without denoising 27.86 / 26.72. The text's percentages don't match its table, and no ablation isolates the LLM priors. Prompts are not disclosed. — [VESPA](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.20397)
- OpenSight (ECCV 2024) asks GPT-4 "What is the average size of a {CLASS} in the autonomous driving scene?" (e.g., car 1.96 × 4.63 × 1.74 m; pedestrian 0.67 × 0.73 × 1.77 m). A lifted box is kept for its object bank only if w, l and h each fall within (1 ± τ) of the prior, τ = 0.2. Ablation (mAP near / mid / far): baseline 27.1 / 24.8 / 22.1; +temporal 27.4 / 25.9 / 23.0; +spatial 27.5 / 26.0 / 22.8; both 28.0 / 26.5 / 23.5. — [OpenSight](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.08876v1); [ECCV 2024 poster](https://eccv2024.ecva.net/virtual/2024/poster/2618)
- DFU3D (ACM MM 2025) uses L-shape BEV boxes and enlarges unrealistically small boxes toward common-sense sizes. Box height comes from the topmost cleaned point. — [DFU3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.20530)
- UNION reports that adding a size prior *hurt*: UNION + size prior 13.0 mAP vs UNION-05pc 25.1; HDBSCAN + size prior 4.9. Here the size prior was used as a class assigner, not a box completer. — [UNION](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15688)

**CPD commonsense prototypes (CVPR 2024)**
- Initial labels: 11 frames (±5) aggregated, with moving points removed via PPScore from non-current frames, then ground removal, DBSCAN and L-shape fitting. Classes come from hand-set size ranges: Vehicle 1 < h ≤ 3, 0.5 < w ≤ 3, 0.5 < l ≤ 8; Pedestrian 0.8 < h ≤ 2.3, 0.2 < w ≤ 1, 0.2 < l ≤ 1; Cyclist 1.4 < h ≤ 2, 0.5 < w ≤ 1, 1 < l ≤ 2.5. Boxes with h ≤ 0.8 are discarded as too small, the rest as too large. — [CPD](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16493)
- Completeness score (CSS) = mean of three terms: a distance score 1 − N(‖c‖); a multi-level occupancy score (share of occupied cells at several grid resolutions); and size similarity to templates via truncated KL (Vehicle [5.06, 1.86, 1.49], Pedestrian [1.0, 1.0, 2.0], Cyclist [1.9, 0.85, 1.8]). Boxes with S ≥ η = 0.8 build the prototypes: high-scoring boxes are averaged per track and their points concatenated. — [CPD](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16493)
- CBR (training-free refinement) matches each label to a prototype by minimum height difference, **re-sizes** it to the prototype's l/w/h, then **re-locates** its boundary and orientation onto the boundary of the densest part of the points (exact corner rule unspecified). Waymo val vehicle 3D AP L2 @ 0.7: 4.77 → 24.04 (+19.27). By component (BEV recall @ 0.5 / 0.7): re-size +3.91 / +3.4; re-localisation +12.68 / +6.43. CST self-training (trained) adds +8.09. No per-distance ablation. — [CPD](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16493); venue confirmed at [arXiv abs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.16493)

**OYSTER track-level size + corner alignment (CVPR 2023)**
- For tracklets with temporal-consistency length ≥ q = 6: set l and w to the **top r-percentile** of all l / w seen in the track (r = 100% PandaSet, 95% AV2). Then anchor on **the box corner closest to ego** ("most observed and likely to be the most reliable") and recompute the centre from that corner, the original heading and the new size. Short tracks (< q) are ignored in training (∅ loss). — [OYSTER](https://research-assets.waabi.ai/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Oyster-paper.pdf); [CVF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2023/html/Zhang_Towards_Unsupervised_Object_Detection_From_LiDAR_Point_Clouds_CVPR_2023_paper.html)
- PandaSet ablation, M5 (no long-track refinement) → M6 (with it): AP@IoU 0.3 / 0.5 / 0.7 = 43.5 / 26.2 / 13.2 → 43.5 / 29.5 / 18.1; recall@0.7 20.6 → 28.1. **But distance-to-collision AP fell** (e.g., ΔDTC 0.5 m: 45.2 → 41.1), which the authors attribute to "increasing the bounding box size with inaccurate heading". — [OYSTER](https://research-assets.waabi.ai/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Oyster-paper.pdf)

**Point completion (ZOPP, arXiv 2411.05311, Nov 2024; no venue on the page)**
- The completion network is **trained**: a PointNet-style encoder plus a CLIP text embedding produce a sparse geometry and then dense points; Chamfer loss; trained 100 epochs on WOD train. Static objects: points aggregated in world frame → L-shape → drop outside points → FPS → L-shape again, then one box mapped back to each frame. Dynamic objects: per-frame boxes, an anchor from the spread of those boxes, a refined L-shape, then linear + Kalman smoothing. — [ZOPP](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.05311)
- WOD val vehicle AP (L1) 0–30 / 30–50 / 50+ m: 42.31 / 35.14 / 29.89, a 29.35% relative drop from near to far (Voxel-RCNN 36.12%, PV-RCNN 37.05%). Filtering + completion together add +26.2 / +12.5 / +11.4 recall (Veh / Ped / Cyc). The completion module's isolated gain is not reported. About 20% of objects have 1–4 m centre error. — [ZOPP](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.05311)

**SDF shape-prior fitting (SLF, arXiv 2407.11382, 2024)**
- Losses: Dice between the SAM mask and the occlusion-filtered rendered silhouette (masks depth-sorted by the median depth of their LiDAR points); an L1 term on the SDF at frustum points plus distance to the first ray–surface hit; a ground term (centre height minus half the height vs a RANSAC plane). Weights not given. Prior: PCA (d = 5) over 79 Apollo-Car3D vehicles. Adam, lr 0.1, 150 iterations, all instances of a scene in one batch (~5× faster). — [SLF](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.11382v2)
- KITTI val AP3D@0.5 (E / M / H): SLF 87.9 / 88.2 / 83.9 vs FGR 91.3 / 77.8 / 70.6. Energy ablation: LiDAR only 70.5 / 64.8 / 62.9; + mask 87.3 / 88.0 / 83.7; + ground 87.9 / 88.2 / 83.9. Mask alone is unstable (~6–7 AP). — [SLF](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.11382v2)
- Beam ablation: 32 beams 81.3 / 77.5 / 77.3; 16 → 80.7 / 76.3 / 70.0; 8 → 75.1 / 60.0 / 56.7. Failure modes: front/rear confusion under sparse LiDAR; worse on distant objects (qualitative only); one CAD set per class. Single image, no multi-view or temporal handling. On nuScenes: SLF 57.2 mAP vs MTrans 45.9 vs FGR 11.6. — [SLF](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.11382v2)

**Track-level / static aggregation (supervised refiners, for magnitude)**
- 3DAL (Waymo, 2021), static-vehicle box accuracy at 3D IoU 0.7 / 0.8: single-frame detector 67.17 / 36.61; 4-frame detector 73.96 / 43.56; object-centric with the whole track 82.28 / 56.92. Dynamic: 80.07 / 57.71 → 85.67 / 65.77 (whole track). The paper notes "dynamic vehicles have a higher accuracy because they are closer to the sensor than static ones." — [3DAL, arXiv 2103.05073](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.05073)
- MS3D static-vehicle refinement (KBF over H history frames), Waymo AP_BEV / AP_3D: 1-frame 57.22 / 38.67; H = 4 62.06 / 40.89; H = 16 62.32 / 43.33; one box for all frames 62.32 / 43.12. H = 16 was chosen because one box for every frame can exclude car points under ego-pose drift. — [MS3D, arXiv 2304.02431](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.02431)

### Inferences
- The fused-pair vs lifted-car gap (w 1.68 vs 0.83; l 3.96 vs 2.26) is OYSTER's tight-box failure. CPD's ablation says the anchor matters more than the size (re-localisation +12.7 BEV recall vs re-size +3.9), so inflating a far car box *about its centroid* would recover little. Grow it away from the visible surface: VESPA's "outward from ego", OYSTER's closest-to-ego corner, CPD's densest boundary.
- Heading is the weak link: OYSTER's DTC drop came from enlarging boxes with the wrong heading. With ~15 points on one face, L-shape fitting can't tell length from width. Track motion (VESPA's > 0.5 m/s rule), the camera mask's aspect, or SLF's mask term have to pick the long axis before a car prior is applied. With a VLP-32C at 20 Hz, a moving car's track heading is far better constrained than any one frame's.
- A cheap frame-level rule set that follows the sources: (1) L-shape fit with variance or closeness, not area minimisation, on sparse returns; (2) if the box falls short of the car prior, grow l/w to the prior from the ego-nearest corner or visible edge (VESPA / OYSTER); (3) then, in `tracking`, replace per-frame sizes with a robust track size (OYSTER top 95–100th percentile, or the MS3D KDE peak) and one box per static object (3DAL / MS3D H = 16).
- SLF is the principled frame-level completion, but on WATO's far cars (VLP-32C, ~9–15 points beyond 15 m) the beam ablation and front/rear confusion suggest it gains little over prior-plus-anchor unless track heading or multi-view masks constrain it.

### Gaps
- No source compares L-shape + prior, CPD-style prototypes, ZOPP completion and SLF on the same data, binned by distance or point count. No "best for far one-sided cars, by X AP" number exists.
- VESPA's LLM prompts and the effect of the priors alone are not reported. Its ablation text and table disagree.
- The SLF follow-ups and comparisons from 2025–2026 I searched for did not surface; none found.
- CPD's exact re-localisation rule (which corner or edge, how bins are formed) is not specified on the HTML page.

## Q2. Multi-source fusion: MS3D / MS3D++ KDE box fusion, Fusion4DAL, WBF, label models / learned source reliability without ground truth; fusion of geometry-only clusters with camera-lifted instances

### Takeaway
MS3D's KBF is a per-attribute weighted Gaussian KDE over **clusters formed by centroid radius, not IoU**. It *selects* the centroid and heading at the density peak rather than averaging them, and weights each box by score × a hand-set per-source weight. It beat WBF and NMS by ~2–4 AP3D, mostly at range. It needs several boxes per object (originally > 4 within 2 m), so two sources in one frame give it almost nothing to work with; it pays off across TTA, many detectors, or many frames of a static object. Fusion4DAL is a closed-access, *trained* LiDAR–camera offline detector, not a proposal-fusion rule. I found no label-model (Dawid–Skene / Snorkel) work for 3D boxes. Published LiDAR-cluster + camera-lifted fusion is crude: LiSe takes a union with a 10 m distance gate; DFU3D fuses at the point level.

### Cited Findings
**MS3D / MS3D++ KDE Box Fusion (KBF)**
- Original MS3D (arXiv 2304.02431, 2023): Gaussian kernel per box parameter, weighted by predicted confidence. The KDE runs on centroid, dimensions and score, and each takes the PDF peak. For heading the KDE runs on sin θ and the most likely original heading is kept ("we choose the box heading with the highest likelihood"), because a high rotation bandwidth would pick an in-between orientation. A KD-tree on centroids does matching: an object is present if "there are more than 4 box proposals with their centroids within 2m of each other." Each detector runs 4 TTA permutations (none, RWF, RWR, RWF+RWR). — [MS3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.02431)
- Fusion ablation (Waymo target, IoU 0.7), AP_BEV / AP_3D and AP_3D at [0, 30) / [30, 50) / [50, ∞) m: NMS 57.93 / 34.88 (57.13 / 24.01 / 7.59); WBF-C 53.27 / 36.55 (67.17 / 32.74 / 8.33); WBF-P 54.44 / 36.75 (67.43 / 33.32 / 8.92); **KBF 57.22 / 38.67 (68.89 / 33.93 / 10.40)**. NMS keeps a slightly higher AP_BEV. — [MS3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.02431)
- Code (`pcdet/utils/box_fusion_utils.py`): `kbf(boxes, box_weights, bw_c=1.0, bw_dim=2.0, bw_ry=0.1, bw_cls=0.5, bw_score=2.0)` over `scipy.stats.gaussian_kde`. Clusters come from `cKDTree.query_ball_point` on 3D centroids with a per-class radius, and clusters with fewer than `discard` unique centroids are skipped. Box weight = score × per-source per-class weight (default 1). **Dimensions are fused unweighted**; centroid, heading, class and score are weighted. If the centroid covariance determinant is < 1e-7, xy and z get separate KDEs. The centroid is a sample at the KDE peak ("aggregating can lead to odd centering"). Dims and score take the KDE peak value; class is the peak sample or the majority. Fused boxes then go through NMS (`nms_thresh` default 0.05). The 0/1/2-box special cases compare a tuple to an int and never trigger. — [MS3D code](https://github.com/darrenjkt/MS3D/blob/main/pcdet/utils/box_fusion_utils.py)
- MS3D++ (arXiv 2308.05988, 2023) sets per-source weights by hand from LiDAR-density similarity to the target (for a Waymo target, Lyft detectors above nuScenes; for nuScenes, Waymo = Lyft) and drops a weak detector (IA-SSD). It drops nuScenes-trained pedestrian predictions because they added false positives. Example, Waymo target, vehicle AP3D at 0–30 / 30–50 / 50–80 m: Lyft-only 4-detector ensemble 80.6 / 54.6 / 28.0; Lyft + nuScenes 82.6 / 52.7 / 25.1; with Lyft weighted up 82.6 / 55.7 / 27.8 (column alignment uncertain in the extraction). — [MS3D++](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.05988)
- MS3D++ pseudo-label quality over self-training rounds 1 → 4, vehicle precision / recall by range: 0–30 m 0.962 → 0.944 / 0.896 → 0.912; 30–50 m 0.934 → 0.893 / 0.648 → 0.763; 50–80 m 0.913 → 0.851 / 0.333 → 0.514. Labelling cost about 20 h for 20k Waymo frames, up to 11 h of it in the tracker. No GPU needed outside the detectors. — [MS3D++](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.05988)

**WBF usage elsewhere**
- 3DAL ensembles TTA predictions with weighted box fusion. DetZero fuses its forward- and reverse-order tracks with WBF. — [3DAL](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.05073); [DetZero](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2306.06023)

**Fusion4DAL (IJCV 2025)**
- Zhiyuan Yang, Xuekuan Wang, Wei Zhang, Xiao Tan, Jincheng Lu, Jingdong Wang, Errui Ding, Cairong Zhao; IJCV 133(7): 3951–3969 (2025); DOI 10.1007/s11263-025-02370-1. — [DBLP](https://dblp.dagstuhl.de/pid/180/5531.html); [Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11263-025-02370-1)
- From search summaries of the abstract: a multi-modal mixed feature fusion (MMFF) module creates **virtual points on a dense 3D grid**, merges them with real LiDAR points and extracts dense point-level image features against LiDAR sparsity. AMiner lists the keywords "global point attention" and "virtual point loss". The authors claim state of the art among offline 3D detectors on nuScenes and Waymo. — [Springer listing via search](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11263-025-02370-1); [AMiner record](https://resource.aminer.org/pub/6845189f163c01c8506d6f28/fusion-dal-offline-multi-modal-d-object-detection-for-d-auto-labeling)
- Semantic Scholar: abstract elided by the publisher, **no open-access PDF, no arXiv ID**, 5 citations. — [Semantic Scholar API](https://api.semanticscholar.org/graph/v1/paper/DOI:10.1007/s11263-025-02370-1?fields=title,abstract,year,venue,openAccessPdf,externalIds,citationCount)

**LiDAR clusters + camera-lifted instances**
- LiSe (ECCV 2024, "Approaching Outside"). LiDAR branch: ppScore-weighted graph DBSCAN, discarding clusters whose top K = 20% of points have ppScore > α = 0.7, then L-shape. Image branch: GroundingDINO → SAM mask → points in the mask → region growing → tight box. **Fusion = union** of all LiDAR boxes plus image boxes ≥ d_min = 10 m from ego (best d_min in their sweep). No NMS or IoU rule is described. Detection after self-training, AP_BEV / AP_3D @ IoU 0.25, 0–80 m: LiDAR-only 11.8 / 6.6; image-only (likely row) 12.4 / 4.7; fused 16.2 / 9.1; full 19.8 / 11.4. At 50–80 m: 1.5 / 0.3 → 3.4 / 0.9 fused. — [LiSe](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.08569v1); [arXiv abs (ECCV acceptance)](https://www.arxiv.org/abs/2407.08569)
- DFU3D (ACM MM 2025) fuses at the **data level**: LiDAR points take SEEM instance labels, and image pixels are lifted with DepthAnything into pseudo points. A local depth-aware ball-query filter (λ = 0.01) and a global statistical-outlier filter clean them; then L-shape, small-box enlargement, and an iterative refit that keeps a new box only if its 3D IoU with same-class boxes is < 0.2. Ablation "All" AP: real points 8.5; pseudo points 7.5; fused 9.6; + local filter 18.8; + both filters 24.5; + dynamic self-evolution 28.4. Class-aware mAP 28.4 vs UNION 25.1 (vehicle 32.3 vs 31.0; pedestrian 37.7 vs 44.2). Class-agnostic AP_3D by range: 29.4 / 9.1 / 2.8 (near / mid / far). — [DFU3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.20530)
- VESPA merges proposals across cameras when the classes match and they share LiDAR points, or both sit in the outer 15% of adjacent images. Points, features and scores are averaged. — [VESPA](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.20397)
- OpenSight's temporal awareness projects frame t ± 1 boxes into frame t by pose. Unmatched projected boxes are added as missed detections; overlapping ones are NMS'd and the union kept (thresholds unstated). — [OpenSight](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.08876v1)

**Label models / unsupervised source reliability**
- Searches found Dawid–Skene-style aggregation only for human annotators: crowdsourced or medical multi-annotator boxes, and a framework for aggregating complex annotations including boxes. Nothing estimates 3D detector or source reliability without ground truth. — [search: arXiv 2303.16507 and related](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2303.16507)
- MS3D++ sets source weights by hand from sensor similarity, not by estimation. — [MS3D++](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.05988)

### Inferences
- KBF's preconditions (≥ 5 boxes within 2 m, from TTA × many detectors) don't hold for wato_world's two per-frame sources. With 1–2 boxes per cluster it reduces to "pick one". Its useful parts are the rules: select rather than average the centroid and heading; fuse dims as a density peak; weight by score × source weight; cluster by centroid radius rather than IoU. The radius rule tolerates a one-sided lifted box whose IoU with the full box is low. Point-sharing already does this job in wato_world's fusion.
- The low per-frame overlap (pairs/lidar_mos 0.11–0.56) suggests fusing over a track: a track counts as supported by both sources if lidar_mos matches it in some frames and lifted in others. MS3D's static H-frame KBF and 3DAL's one box per static track both work at that granularity.
- None of the published fusers estimate per-source reliability from the data. A label-free estimate (cross-source agreement, temporal persistence, free-space consistency as noisy "labelers" in a Dawid–Skene / Snorkel label model) looks open. That matches candidate contribution #1 in `docs/research/novelty_positioning.md`.
- LiSe's 10 m gate (prefer LiDAR boxes near, image boxes far) is the opposite of wato_world's problem: its far image boxes are useful because they *add recall*, not because their geometry is good. Its 50–80 m AP_3D is under 1.

### Gaps
- Fusion4DAL's full text, fusion design, temporal window and numbers: Springer paywall, no arXiv, no code found. Everything above comes from abstract-level summaries.
- MS3D++'s numeric s_pos, N_pos, tracker thresholds and static/dynamic thresholds were not in the HTML I read; they may be in the repo's config YAMLs, which I did not open.
- No published label model for 3D proposal sources.

## Q3. How do methods reject false proposals before tracking (point count, size, ground contact, occlusion, free-space / ray casting)?

### Takeaway
Published rejection is mostly simple: a minimum point count (MS3D: ≥ 1 point in the box), class size windows (CPD; OpenSight ±20% of an LLM prior), mask NMS (VESPA IoA > 0.5), static-cluster or ephemerality filters (ppScore), appearance-cluster "mobility" (UNION), and above all **temporal persistence**, i.e. dropping short tracks (OYSTER q = 6; MS3D N_pos). I found no auto-labeling paper that rejects a box because LiDAR rays pass through it. Visibility from raycasting is used as a *detector input* (WYSIWYG, CVPR 2020). DetZero's nested-box overlap-ratio filter and SLF's depth-ordered masks are the closest published tools for "hidden car boxed on its occluder".

### Cited Findings
- MS3D: drop pseudo-labels with < 1 LiDAR point inside. A track becomes a label only after N_pos detections above s_pos; fine-tuning uses s > 0.6. Propagated boxes decay by β = 0.95 per frame with a floor of α = 0.7, applied to tracks with > 7 detections. — [MS3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.02431)
- MS3D++: poles score 0.3–0.6, like real pedestrians, so motion separates pedestrians from static pole-like objects (static pedestrians admitted only after a few rounds). Static vehicle tracks use a high BEV-IoU association so parked cars are not called dynamic. A tracklet is dynamic if either its 1-frame or 16-frame track is, so temporarily stopped vehicles are not extrapolated. — [MS3D++](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.05988); [MS3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.02431)
- CPD size windows: Vehicle 1 < h ≤ 3 m, 0.5 < w ≤ 3, 0.5 < l ≤ 8; h ≤ 0.8 discarded. — [CPD](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16493)
- OpenSight keeps a box for its bank only if w, l, h are each within ±20% of the GPT-4 prior. — [OpenSight](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.08876v1)
- VESPA: GroundingDINO confidence ≥ 0.3; a custom mask NMS suppresses a mask whose intersection-over-area with a higher-confidence mask exceeds 0.5, otherwise trims it; DBSCAN largest component with eps = LLM class width; ground removal within 30 cm of a sector-wise RANSAC ground over 5 sweeps. — [VESPA](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.20397)
- ZOPP's parallax-occlusion filter: because the LiDAR sits higher than the cameras, **background points project onto foreground pixels**. Per mask, processed near to far, a sliding kernel (size 15, steps 10 / 5) flags pixels where the depth ratio (max − min)/min > 0.25; "far" points inside the rectangle spanned by the near points are removed. This raised vehicle segmentation 51.6 → 54.2. — [ZOPP](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.05311)
- SLF handles occlusion by sorting masks by the median depth of their LiDAR points and occlusion-filtering each rendered silhouette, so only the visible part is compared to the mask. — [SLF](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.11382v2)
- DetZero's tracker "removes boxes nested inside others, where IoU fails" (overlap-ratio filter), and tracks high-score boxes (> 0.1 with enough LiDAR points) first, then low-score. — [DetZero](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2306.06023)
- OYSTER: tracking plus short-tracklet filtering (q = 6) raised AP@0.3 / 0.5 / 0.7 from 26.6 / 14.5 / 4.1 to 32.3 / 17.8 / 7.5 (M3 → M4). — [OYSTER](https://research-assets.waabi.ai/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Oyster-paper.pdf)
- UNION drops proposals in DINOv2 appearance clusters (K-means K1 = 20) where < 5% of members are dynamic. Ablation: HDBSCAN 13.8 AP / 15.7 NDS; + motion 14.2 / 19.8; + appearance clustering 39.5 / 31.7. — [UNION](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15688)
- LiSe discards LiDAR clusters whose top 20% of points have ppScore > 0.7 (persistent, so static background); image boxes get no filter. — [LiSe](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.08569v1)
- UniLiPs labels a point moving only if it has no static-map correspondence (30 cm match radius) and has enough nearby moving candidates (the HTML renders this as "22 within 11 m", probably 2 within 1 m). Occlusion tolerance grows with range, T(r) = 1 + αr. — [UniLiPs](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05105)
- Free space: WYSIWYG (Hu, Ziglar, Held, Ramanan, CVPR 2020) argues LiDAR is 2.5D and "also reveal[s] free space between the sensor and each measured point". It raycasts a voxelised visibility map and feeds it to a detector as a second stream; a third-party review quotes +4.5% mAP over PointPillars on nuScenes test (not verified against the paper). — [arXiv 1912.04986](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.04986); [CVF PDF](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2020/papers/Hu_What_You_See_is_What_You_Get_Exploiting_Visibility_for_CVPR_2020_paper.pdf); [review](https://liner.com/review/what-you-see-is-what-you-get-exploiting-visibility-for)
- A mapless dynamic-object detector treats sensor-to-return ray paths as free space and tests whether reference rays cross a query surface. That is the primitive a "box must not contain seen-through space" test would use. — [arXiv 1809.06972](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.06972)

### Inferences
- The 3.28 m lifted car boxes would fail CPD's vehicle window (h ≤ 3) or OpenSight's ±20% (1.74 × 1.2 ≈ 2.09 m). A class height cap plus re-fitting height from points within a few cm of the ground grid up to a car-plausible height (DFU3D-style topmost point *after* outlier filtering) are both training-free. VESPA-style eps = class width (≈ 1.7–1.9 m for cars) instead of 2.0 m would also cut bleed, at some cost to recall along VLP-16 rings.
- For "hidden car boxed on its occluder", the published tools are: (1) SLF's depth ordering of masks, so a nearer mask claims the shared points; (2) DetZero's nested-box ratio, which drops a box mostly inside another box; (3) ZOPP's parallax filter, which targets the opposite case, background points in a foreground mask. Locally, wato_world's measurement already shows the hidden instance's LiDAR points sit at the occluder's depth (median 0.0 m). A rule that **drops a lifted instance whose points mostly fall inside another, nearer same-frame box** follows DetZero's idea and doesn't depend on the far-range depth-map error.
- A free-space test fits wato_world's existing occlusion metric. Over a box's angular footprint, count returns *behind* the box (rays passed through it) vs returns on or in front of it; a box with many pass-through rays is in seen-through space. I found no published threshold, so it would need its own measurement doc. The metric is per sensor: use the sensor-origin-correct sweep (per-corner topics, as CLAUDE.md requires).
- Temporal persistence (OYSTER q = 6, MS3D N_pos) is the most-used published filter, and it belongs to `tracking`, not proposal_generation. This supports keeping proposal_generation high-recall.

### Gaps
- No auto-labeling paper found with an explicit ray-casting "box contains seen-through space" rejection and a measured threshold.
- No published rejection rule for a hidden-object box sitting on its occluder, beyond depth-ordered masks and nested-box filters.
- MS3D's numeric s_pos / N_pos are not given in the papers' text.

## Q4. How do UniLiPs and UNION produce boxes; do parked objects get boxes?

### Takeaway
UniLiPs (arXiv 2601.05105, Jan 2026) boxes **moving points only**: map-differenced across 3 aligned scans, HDBSCAN, minimum-enclosing cuboid with a size floor and PCA yaw, a constant-velocity-with-yaw Kalman filter, then spline smoothing per track. Parked cars stay in the static map, so presumably no box (not stated). UNION (arXiv 2405.15688, v3 Feb 2025) clusters **all** non-ground points over 15 aggregated scans, marks clusters dynamic by ICP-Flow motion ≥ 0.5 m, and then **gives parked objects boxes** by keeping every proposal (static or dynamic) in an appearance cluster that is ≥ 5% dynamic. Appearance clustering is where most of UNION's AP comes from (14.2 → 39.5).

### Cited Findings
- UniLiPs: a point is moving if it has no correspondence in the refined static map and has nearby moving support. 3 consecutive scans are aligned and only moving points kept. HDBSCAN clusters, a minimum-enclosing cuboid with a minimum size (unstated), PCA initial yaw. Kalman tracker with constant velocity including yaw dynamics; spline optimisation per trajectory (yaw as sin/cos bases, x/y separately). — [UniLiPs](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05105)
- UniLiPs box ablation: full 31.0 mAP / 45.2 NDS; without spline 23.9 / 33.2; without IWU 11.7 / 30.8. Highway: UniLiPs 31.0 / 45.2 vs LISO 21.1 / 40.9 vs ICP-Flow 7.2 / 11.4. BEV AP at 25 / 50 / 70 m: oracle 35.6 / 33.5 / 32.4 vs UniLiPs (20% GT + 80% pseudo) 31.0 / 29.4 / 29.2. — [UniLiPs](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05105)
- UniLiPs doesn't address parked vehicles; boxes come only from moving-object segmentation, and static points stay in the map. — [UniLiPs](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05105)
- UNION: RANSAC ground (5 cm inlier, keep > 30 cm above); ±7 scans aggregated (15 total); HDBSCAN (min cluster 16, selection eps 0.50 m). Modified ICP-Flow (2D translation + yaw), dynamic if it moves ≥ 0.50 m. DINOv2 ViT-L/14 features averaged over the projected points; K-means K1 = 20 over all proposals; a cluster is mobile if ≥ 5% of its members are dynamic. Mobile proposals, **static ones included**, get boxes from MODEST's fitter. Class-agnostic nuScenes: HDBSCAN 13.8 AP; + motion 14.2; + appearance 39.5 (above 1% supervised 27.8, below 10% supervised 61.2). Cyclist AP ≈ 0. — [UNION](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15688)
- UNION's stated limitations: rare mobile classes may be merged with background and discarded; orientation is hard, especially for pedestrians. — [UNION](https://arxiv.org/html/2405.15688)

### Inferences
- wato_world's lidar_mos source is UniLiPs-shaped (Step E IWU → Step F HDBSCAN on movers), so like UniLiPs it gives no parked-car boxes. Parked cars come only from the lifted source. That fits the measurement that most lifted boxes have no lidar_mos partner.
- UNION's mechanism suggests a training-free way to add parked cars from LiDAR geometry: give a static cluster a box when its appearance, or its lifted class, matches clusters that move. wato_world already has the class signal (perception_2d / lifted instances), so a static cluster that a lifted car instance overlaps could be promoted. In effect this is UNION's appearance clustering with SAM2 classes instead of K-means.
- UniLiPs's spline gain (+7.1 mAP) is more evidence that smoothing poses and sizes over a track is worth more than per-frame fitting.

### Gaps
- UniLiPs's supplement (spline details, minimum box size) was not available. Its moving-point neighbourhood rule is garbled in the HTML.
- UNION reports no separate static vs dynamic recall.

## Q5. Is per-frame box generation the right granularity, or do strong methods defer box fitting to the track level?

### Takeaway
Strong offline labelers generate per-frame proposals for recall and **fix size, and for static objects the whole box, at the track level**. The gains are large and consistent: 3DAL static vehicles +15 points of box accuracy at IoU 0.7 from whole-track fitting; MS3D static +4.7 AP3D (H = 16); DetZero +7.7 APH from track-level geometry, position and confidence refiners; UniLiPs +7.1 mAP from spline smoothing; VESPA −21 mAP without tracking; OYSTER +4.9 AP@0.7 from track size. The per-frame source of ground truth is mostly gone by the time the box is final.

### Cited Findings
- 3DAL (2021) splits static from dynamic first: "it is preferred to assign a single 3D bounding box to a static object rather than separate boxes in different frames to avoid jittering". A linear classifier on heuristic track features reaches 99%+ motion-state accuracy for vehicles. Static: points merged in world frame → one box → mapped to each frame. Context ablation (static / dynamic, Acc@0.7): [−0, +0] 78.13 / 80.65; [−2, +2] 79.60 / 84.34; [−5, +5] 80.48 / 85.10; whole track 82.28 / 85.67. Human study (5 WOD sequences, vehicle 3D AP@0.7 / 0.8): human 86.45 / 60.49 vs 3DAL 85.37 / 56.93. — [3DAL](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.05073)
- DetZero (arXiv 2306.06023, 2023): offline tracker → geometry refiner (pool all track points in the box frame, predict one size averaged and assigned to every frame) → position refiner (per-frame centre offsets, 12 × 30° heading bins) → confidence refiner (S = √(S_cls² + S_iou²)). Vehicle L2 APH @ 0.7: tracker 75.24; + GRM 77.17; + PRM 78.55; GRM + PRM 81.01; + CRM 82.92. Human study vehicle 3D AP@0.7 / 0.8: DetZero 90.24 / 67.61 vs human 86.45 / 60.49. These refiners are trained networks. — [DetZero](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/2306.06023)
- MS3D: static vehicles get KBF over H = 16 frames (AP3D 38.67 → 43.33); tracks are withheld until N_pos confident detections and then applied retroactively to all past and future frames; only confident dynamic tracks are interpolated or extrapolated. — [MS3D](https://arxiv.org/html/2304.02431); [MS3D++](https://arxiv.org/html/2308.05988)
- ZOPP fits static objects once on world-aggregated points; dynamic objects per frame, then smoothed with linear fitting and a Kalman filter. — [ZOPP](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.05311)
- OYSTER sets track-level l/w (top r-percentile) with corner alignment, and ignores short tracks. — [OYSTER](https://research-assets.waabi.ai/wp-content/uploads/2023/05/Oyster-paper.pdf)
- CPD builds prototypes per tracking ID and smooths sizes temporally in its initial stage. — [CPD](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16493)
- VESPA without tracking: 46.76 → 25.48 mAP; its tracker matches DINOv2 mask embeddings (same class, mutual best, LLM-velocity gate) and uses ICP on matched clusters for velocity and heading. — [VESPA](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.20397)
- UniLiPs: spline optimisation per trajectory, 23.9 → 31.0 mAP. — [UniLiPs](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.05105)
- Offline-Poly (arXiv 2602.13772, 2026) frames offline 3D MOT as "a critical component of the 4D auto-labeling (4DAL) process"; not read in detail. — [Offline-Poly](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.13772v1)

### Inferences
- For wato_world: proposal_generation should optimise per-frame *recall* plus a reliable *anchor*: visible corner or edge, heading evidence, observed vs prior extent. Final l/w/h, and one box per static object, belong in `tracking` / `label_refinement`, which is what every strong method does. Per-frame fused-pair rates and lifted-car sizes then matter less than whether each frame's proposal carries enough to anchor a track-level box.
- Training-free track-level options, cheapest first: OYSTER percentile size + nearest-corner anchor; MS3D H-frame KDE for static objects; 3DAL-style motion-state split (a linear rule on track displacement) with one world-frame box per static track fitted on the aggregated points (L-shape, or SLF on many frames' masks). Learned refiners (3DAL, DetZero, ZOPP completion) need labels and are deferred.
- Sizing at the track level also changes the multi-source fusion question (Q2): agreement can be counted per track rather than per frame. That softens the small per-frame overlap and gives a natural unit for label-free source-reliability estimates.
- Caveat from OYSTER: track-level enlargement with a wrong heading hurt the DTC metrics, so track-level size should go with track-level heading (motion direction for movers).

### Gaps
- CTRL ("Once Detected, Never Lost", track-centric offline detection) surfaced only as a title in a related-papers listing; its method and numbers were not verified.
- DetZero's venue is not stated on the ar5iv page (often cited as ICCV 2023; unverified here).
- No training-free method was found with a per-distance ablation of track-level vs frame-level sizing.
